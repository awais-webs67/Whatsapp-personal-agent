## How to order

3. After the payment is verified the credentials, invite link or activation steps appear on the private order page and are emailed. Automatic payment methods verify in seconds; manual wallet transfers are checked within 5 to 15 minutes in working hours.

## Warranty and refunds
- Every product page states its warranty before purchase.
- Full Warranty (FW): covered for the whole plan period (for example 27 to 30 days on a 1-month plan). If it stops working while the customer follows the product rules, it is repaired or replaced free.
- First-Login Warranty: covers the first login within the time stated on the product (for example 1 to 24 hours after delivery). After that, platform changes are not covered.
- No Warranty (NW): some discounted or promotional items; always labelled.
- Replacement or refund applies when: the credentials or link do not work on first use and the customer reports it within the warranty window with the Order ID; an FW product stops during the covered period and cannot be restored in reasonable time; the payment was verified but stock ran out before delivery; or the customer paid twice for the same order.
- Not covered: reports after the warranty period; NW items after a working delivery; problems caused by the customer changing passwords, emails or billing, or sharing credentials against the product rules; change of mind after the credentials or key were revealed.
- For a warranty claim the customer should send the Order ID (SMT-XXXXXX) and a screenshot or short description of the error.
- On shared or profile-based plans, customers must use only their own profile and must not change the account email, password, billing or security settings unless the product page says so. Breaking the product rules voids the warranty.
- SellMeTools is an independent marketplace, not affiliated with the software brands it sells.
